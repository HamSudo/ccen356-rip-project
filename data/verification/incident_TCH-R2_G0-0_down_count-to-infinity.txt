Incident during the T3 tests, 6 Nov 2026 (TCH-R2 clock ~18:20-18:23)
=====================================================================

What happened: about a minute after T3-run2 finished (its after-snapshot was
still normal), the cable TCH-R2 G0/0 <-> SW-TCH Fa0/7 came loose (physical
'down', not 'administratively down'). TCH-R2 lost its trunk, so its LAN
192.168.20.0/24 and its link to LIB-R1 (G0/0.12) went down. The same cable had
come loose once before, earlier the same day.

Why it is worth keeping: TCH-R2 then learned a route to its OWN LAN back from
LAB-R3 at metric 3. LIB-R1 still advertised a stale 192.168.20.0/24 (its route
had not yet hit the 180 s invalid timer), LAB-R3 passed it on with metric 2,
and TCH-R2 accepted it at metric 3: the start of a count-to-infinity loop,
which RIP only stops at metric 16 (or via timers / poisoning). Evidence for
report section 7 (Limitations of RIP).

At the same time, from LIB-R1:  ping 192.168.20.1 -> 0/3 (its route to
192.168.20.0/24 via 10.0.12.2 was stale: the next hop's trunk was down).

After the cable was replaced (all interfaces up again), LIB-R1 still showed
  R  192.168.20.0/24 is possibly down
i.e. the route had passed the 180 s invalid timer and was in hold-down, so
LIB-R1 kept distrusting it for a while even though TCH-R2 was back. A second
real example of RIP's timers delaying recovery.
